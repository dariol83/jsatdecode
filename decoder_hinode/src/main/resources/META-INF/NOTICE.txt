Hinode timing recovery adapts the ClockRecoveryMMComplex equations from
jradio 1.121 (https://github.com/dernasherbrezon/jradio), licensed under the
Apache License, Version 2.0. The MMSE interpolator and CCSDS RS implementation
are used from the jradio dependency, together with two independent long-form
DcBlocker instances for optional initial I/Q DC removal. See licenses/jradio-LICENSE.txt.
Receiver parameters and RRC coefficients originate from this repository's
examples/decoders/hinode_s_tlm reference implementation.
